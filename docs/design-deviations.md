# Design deviations from the POC wireframes

The 12-page wireframe PDF (`docs/spec/EdgeVitals-POC-Wireframes.pdf`) is the visual reference. The app follows its layout, copy and colours. These are the places where it deliberately differs, and why.

| PDF page | Wireframe                                                                             | App                                                                                                                                                                          | Reason                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2 → 3    | Consent goes straight to "About you".                                                 | A **Create your account** screen sits between them.                                                                                                                          | An account is needed to keep the baseline and to export or delete data (FR-001). It comes after consent so nothing is stored before the person agrees. |
| 2        | "[HOSTING REGION]" placeholder.                                                       | Shows the configured `HOSTING_REGION`, or "hosting region not configured".                                                                                                   | The API refuses to start in production without a region, so the placeholder cannot ship.                                                               |
| 1, 8     | "Heart rate, HRV, breathing, SpO₂ and blood pressure"; "Measuring all 5 vital signs". | "Measuring 3 of 5 vital signs"; SpO₂ and blood pressure show **Not available**. The welcome screen says so.                                                                  | A phone camera cannot measure SpO₂ or blood pressure with any validated method (ADR 0004). Showing numbers would be fabrication.                       |
| 8        | "[TIME] left" countdown.                                                              | Elapsed time and a progress bar that only moves with usable signal.                                                                                                          | Scan length depends on signal quality (target 45 s of usable signal, stop at 90 s). A fixed countdown would be wrong.                                  |
| 8        | "Skip to results (prototype only)".                                                   | "Show demo results (simulated)", only when the server has demo mode on. Demo results carry a banner and **Demo** tags and never count toward the baseline, scores or trends. | Keeps a way to demo the flow without presenting simulated values as readings. Demo mode cannot be enabled in production.                               |
| 9        | Typical/Higher tags on every vital, including SpO₂ and blood pressure.                | Tags only for metrics with a valid reading and a ready baseline. Before day 14 the tag is omitted.                                                                           | Tags compare against the person's own baseline, which does not exist yet.                                                                              |
| 5, 9, 10 | No validation note.                                                                   | "Camera estimates are not clinically validated" on readings.                                                                                                                 | Requirement from the metric-availability decision.                                                                                                     |
| 10       | Example values ("Up 34% since you started", scores 78/24/72).                         | Real values from the person's history, or "Building your trend" / "Not yet" until there is enough data.                                                                      | The wireframe numbers are illustrative.                                                                                                                |
| 10, 12   | Blood pressure value and "Slightly higher" tag.                                       | "Not available from the camera".                                                                                                                                             | As above.                                                                                                                                              |
| Today    | No settings entry.                                                                    | A settings icon top-right (profile, schedule, consents, export, delete account, sign out).                                                                                   | Export, deletion and consent withdrawal must be reachable (privacy requirements).                                                                      |

Score wording ("Good", "Calm", "Charged" …) follows the PDF. The score formulas are drafts awaiting review (ADR 0005).
